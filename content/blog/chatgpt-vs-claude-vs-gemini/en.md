## The short answer

There is no universal winner. **ChatGPT** (OpenAI), **Claude** (Anthropic) and **Gemini** (Google) are strong assistants, and their capabilities are updated regularly. The difference usually shows not in "intelligence" but in convenience for a specific task, integrations and ecosystem.

A practical approach: take your real tasks, run them through all three services on free tiers and pick the one whose output needs the least fixing.

## Comparison by task type

| Task | What to look at |
|---|---|
| Writing and emails | Natural style, following tone, fewer clichés |
| Code | Understanding your stack, handling large files, IDE integration |
| Data analysis | Uploading spreadsheets, running calculations, building charts |
| Long documents | Context size, accuracy on details near the end of a file |
| Multimodal | Recognizing images, screenshots, PDFs, voice |
| Up-to-date information | Built-in web search and source citations |

### Writing

All three write well. They differ in manner: one may sound drier, another more "marketing". This is subjective, so compare on your own examples: a client email, a post, a product description.

### Code

All three help write and explain code. For developers, integrations matter more than the chat: editor plugins, agentic tools, repository access. Check which tool fits your workflow best.

### Analysis and long documents

If you often upload contracts, reports or data exports, check two things: how much text fits into one request and how accurately the model answers about details in the middle and end of a document. A large context does not guarantee careful reading.

### Multimodal

All three handle images, screenshots and PDFs, but the feature sets differ: image generation, voice mode, video analysis. Check the current plan descriptions.

## Russian and Uzbek

In **Russian**, all three assistants perform confidently.

With **Uzbek**, results are less consistent: quality depends on the model and the task. Typical issues:

- mixing Latin and Cyrillic scripts;
- wrong apostrophes in o‘ and g‘;
- literal translations from Russian or English instead of natural phrasing;
- errors in specialized terminology.

If Uzbek matters to you, have a native speaker review the texts and compare the assistants specifically on Uzbek tasks.

## Pricing and access

Each service usually offers:

- a **free tier** limited in models and number of requests;
- a **paid subscription** for individual use;
- **team and enterprise plans** with administration and data terms;
- an **API** to embed the model in your products, billed by usage.

Prices and limits change often, so check the official websites for current information. Also verify **availability in your country** and payment options, which can be the deciding factor.

## How to choose: a checklist

1. Write down 5-10 tasks you actually handle every week.
2. Run identical requests through all three services.
3. Evaluate: how many edits are needed, are there invented facts, how convenient is the interface.
4. Test Uzbek and Russian if you need them.
5. Consider the ecosystem: Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, your code editor.
6. For business, study the data handling terms on team plans.

## Common mistakes

- Choosing by reviews and rankings instead of your own tasks.
- Pasting confidential data into personal free accounts.
- Treating the choice as final: models get updated, and the leader for your task can change.

## FAQ

### Can I use several at once?

Yes, and many people do: one assistant for code, another for writing. For a team it is easier to agree on one primary tool, which simplifies access and data management.

### Which assistant is safer for company data?

It depends less on the brand than on the plan. Team and enterprise plans usually give more control over data. Read the terms of the specific plan before uploading internal documents.

### Do I need a paid subscription?

For occasional tasks the free tier is often enough. A subscription makes sense when you hit the limits or need stronger models and features such as file analysis.
