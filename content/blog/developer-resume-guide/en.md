## What makes a resume strong

Recruiters skim resumes quickly, looking for one answer: does this person fit the role? A strong resume is:

- **Short**: one page for junior and mid-level, up to two for experienced engineers.
- **Specific**: results and technologies instead of duties.
- **Tailored**: the key skills from the vacancy are visible immediately.
- **Simple in format**: easy for both a human and an ATS to read.

## Resume structure

A block order that works for most tech roles:

1. **Contacts**: name, city or time zone, email, Telegram or phone, links to GitHub, LinkedIn and portfolio.
2. **Headline**: role and stack, for example "Frontend Developer, React, TypeScript".
3. **Short summary**: 2–3 sentences on what you do and where you are strong.
4. **Skills**: grouped by category — languages, frameworks, databases, tools.
5. **Work experience**: in reverse chronological order.
6. **Projects**: especially important when commercial experience is limited.
7. **Education and courses**: brief.

A photo, age, marital status and a list of hobbies are usually unnecessary unless the employer explicitly asks for them.

## How to phrase achievements

Describe not what you were assigned, but what changed because of your work. A handy formula: **action + what you did + technology + result**.

**Before:**

> Worked on the frontend of an online store. Responsible for layout and bug fixing.

**After:**

> Migrated the online store catalog to React and TypeScript and added lazy image loading, making the catalog page load noticeably faster.

**Before:**

> Wrote APIs.

**After:**

> Built a REST API for a delivery mobile app with Node.js and PostgreSQL and covered the key endpoints with automated tests.

If you have real measurable results — response times, user numbers, less manual work — include them. If you do not have numbers, do not invent them: describing the change works too.

## Tailoring to the vacancy

One generic resume for every job loses. Before you apply:

- list the key technologies and requirements from the posting;
- move relevant skills and projects higher;
- use the same wording as the vacancy, as long as it is true for you;
- remove what is irrelevant to the role.

It takes 10–15 minutes and noticeably increases the chance your resume gets read in full.

## ATS-friendly formatting

An **ATS** (Applicant Tracking System) stores applications and helps recruiters search by keywords. Complex layouts can be parsed incorrectly.

| Do | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Single column, standard headings | Complex multi-column layouts |
| Text, not images | Skills as icons and rating bars |
| PDF or DOCX if requested | Scans and image files |
| Full technology names | Homemade abbreviations |
| One consistent date format | Tables with important text inside |

Drop bars like "JavaScript — 80%": they tell a recruiter nothing and systems cannot read them.

## Common mistakes

- **A list of duties** instead of results.
- **Every technology** you have ever touched.
- **Typos** in technology names: "Javascript", "React.JS", "Postgre".
- **No links** to code even though you are applying as a developer.
- **Too long**: three pages for a junior role.
- **Generic phrases**: "stress-resistant, team player, fast learner".
- **Outdated contacts** or links to empty profiles.

## Quick checklist before sending

- The headline matches the role in the vacancy.
- The key technologies from the posting appear in skills and experience.
- Every experience bullet starts with an action verb.
- GitHub and portfolio links open.
- The file name is clear: "FirstName-LastName-Frontend.pdf".

## FAQ

### Should my resume be in English?

If you apply to international companies or the vacancy is in English — yes. For a local market your native language is often enough, but having an English version is useful.

### What do I write without commercial experience?

Put the projects section above education. Describe pet projects, freelance work, internships and study projects the same way as jobs: the task, the technologies, the result.

### Do I need a cover letter?

If there is a field for it, use it. A few sentences on why this particular company interests you and which experience fits make your application stand out among templated ones.
