## The short answer

**Performance Max (PMax)** is a campaign type where you set the goal and supply the materials, and Google decides where, to whom and what to show. One campaign covers the whole Google Ads inventory: Search, Display, YouTube, Gmail, Discover and Maps.

PMax makes sense when:

- **conversions are tracked accurately** and there are enough of them to learn from;
- you run an **online store with a product feed** in Google Merchant Center, or a business with a clear online action: lead, call, purchase;
- you have **good creative**: copy, images, logos, video;
- you are willing to trade some control for reach.

## How the campaign is built

### Asset groups

An **asset group** is a set of materials for one theme or category: headlines, long headlines, descriptions, images, logos, videos and a final URL. Google assembles ads for each channel from them.

- Create separate asset groups for different product or service categories rather than one for everything.
- Fill in every asset type. Without a video, Google may generate one from your images, and you do not control how it looks.
- Watch the asset group's ad strength and the asset report, and replace weak materials.

### Audience signals

An **audience signal** tells the system where to start looking: your customer lists, website visitors, custom segments based on interests and searches. It is not strict targeting — the campaign can show ads beyond the signal when it finds conversions there. Signals speed up learning, especially early on.

You can also add **search themes** — words and phrases about your business that help the campaign understand which searches are relevant.

### Feeds

For retail the key element is the **product feed** from Merchant Center. Titles, descriptions, prices, availability and images in the feed directly shape your shopping ads. A poor feed means a poor PMax, so clean it up first.

### Channel distribution

Budget is not split between channels in advance. The system moves it to wherever it sees more conversions or value under your bid strategy: Maximize conversions or Maximize conversion value, with an optional target CPA or ROAS.

## Limits in transparency and control

| What you may want | What PMax gives |
|---|---|
| Every search query | Grouped insights by search category, not a full list |
| Bids per channel | The algorithm decides the split |
| Strict audience targeting | Signals only |
| Full placement control | Exclusions exist, but the toolset is narrower than in standalone campaigns |

Two more risks:

- **Brand traffic.** PMax readily picks up searches for your brand that would convert anyway, which flatters its reports. Use brand exclusions and keep a separate brand Search campaign.
- **Final URL expansion.** The campaign may send people to other pages of your site. If you do not want that, turn expansion off or exclude sections such as the blog and careers pages.

## What to prepare before launch

1. **Conversion tracking**, ideally with values: purchase amounts, different values for different lead types.
2. **Only meaningful goals as primary.** If page views or clicks are primary conversions, PMax will chase them.
3. **A budget** that supports a steady flow of conversions. Too small a budget stretches the learning period.
4. **A clean feed or relevant landing pages** for each asset group.
5. **Audience lists:** customers and website visitors to use as signals.
6. **Patience.** After launch and major edits there is a learning period, and frequent changes restart it.

## When PMax is not the best choice

- Few or no conversions, or unreliable tracking.
- You need strict reporting on every search and placement.
- A very narrow niche where manual control over a small set of searches matters — a Search campaign is clearer there.

## FAQ

### Does Performance Max replace Search campaigns?

No. They usually work together: a Search campaign covers core searches with full control, and PMax adds reach across other channels. When a search is identical to a keyword in your Search campaign, the Search campaign generally takes priority.

### How many asset groups should I create?

As many as you have genuinely different lines of business with their own materials and pages. Splitting further does not help, because each group needs data to learn.

### Can I run PMax without a website?

You need a conversion goal you can track. For most businesses that means a website or landing page; without trackable actions the campaign cannot optimize properly.
