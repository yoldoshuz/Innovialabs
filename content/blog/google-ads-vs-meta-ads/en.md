## The short answer

**Google Ads** works with demand that already exists: someone types "air conditioner repair Tashkent" and sees your ad. **Meta Ads** (Facebook and Instagram) works with attention: someone scrolls their feed and you show them something they may want, even though they were not thinking about it.

Hence the main rule: if people actively search for your product, start with Google. If people need to see your product before they want it, start with Meta.

## Intent vs interests

| | Google Ads (search) | Meta Ads |
|---|---|---|
| **How it finds people** | By keywords in the search query | By interests, demographics, behavior, lookalike audiences |
| **User's mindset** | Looking for a solution right now | Relaxing, scrolling |
| **What you do** | Capture existing demand | Create demand and awareness |
| **Audience size** | Limited by search volume | Large, but less "hot" |
| **Key ad asset** | Copy that matches the query | Visuals: photos, video, Reels |

Note that Google is more than search. The Display Network, YouTube and Performance Max also show ads by interests. And Meta has retargeting that follows people who already visited your site. But the core of each platform is intent and interests respectively.

## Ad formats

**Google Ads:**
- **Search ads** — text ads in search results.
- **Display banners** on partner websites.
- **YouTube video ads.**
- **Shopping campaigns** with product cards, prices and images.
- **Performance Max** — an automated campaign across several Google surfaces at once.

**Meta Ads:**
- **Image and video ads in the feed** on Facebook and Instagram.
- **Stories and Reels** — vertical full-screen format.
- **Carousels** — several cards in one ad.
- **Lead Ads** — a lead form inside the app, no website visit needed.
- **Click-to-message ads** — opening Messenger, WhatsApp or Instagram Direct.

## What drives cost

Both platforms run auctions, so there is no universal price per click or impression. Cost depends on:

- **Competition** in your niche and region: the more advertisers want the same audience, the higher the price.
- **Ad quality.** In Google it is Quality Score (relevance, expected CTR, landing page); in Meta it is quality and engagement rankings. Better ads cost less.
- **Campaign objective.** Optimizing for purchases usually costs more than optimizing for reach.
- **Seasonality** and holidays.
- **Landing page.** A slow or confusing site lowers conversion, so each lead costs more even if clicks are cheap.

Comparing platforms by cost per click is pointless. Compare **cost per lead and per customer**, with conversions tracked the same way.

## Which business suits which platform

**Google Ads usually wins when:**
- there is clear search demand: services, repairs, delivery, B2B solutions;
- the need is urgent ("tow truck", "dentist near me");
- buyers compare options and search for a specific model or price.

**Meta Ads usually wins when:**
- the product is visual: fashion, cosmetics, food, interiors, travel;
- it is something new that nobody searches for yet;
- the purchase is emotional or impulsive;
- you want leads to arrive directly in messengers.

**Both together** work when Meta creates interest and Google catches people who later search for your brand or category. To see that combination you need UTM tags and attribution beyond last click.

## How to start without wasting money

1. **Set up analytics and conversions** before launch: GA4 or Yandex Metrica, goals, the Meta pixel.
2. **Start with one channel** that matches your type of demand.
3. **Set a test budget and period** large enough for the algorithm to collect data.
4. **Watch cost per lead and lead quality**, not clicks.
5. Add the second channel once the first works steadily.

## FAQ

### Can I advertise on a small budget?

Yes, but with a small budget focus on one channel and a narrow segment. Spread across several platforms, none of them will collect enough data to optimize.

### Which is better for B2B?

Most start with Google search, where intent shows up in professional queries. Meta helps B2B with retargeting and content promotion, but its job-title targeting is limited.

### Why do Meta ads bring many cheap but irrelevant leads?

Lead Ads take a couple of taps to submit, so people sometimes send them by accident. A qualifying question in the form, optimizing for quality leads and sending deal statuses back to Meta all help.
