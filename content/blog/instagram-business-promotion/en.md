## The short answer

Instagram promotion rests on four pillars:

1. A **profile** that explains in seconds who you are, who you serve and what to do next.
2. **Reels** to reach new people; **Stories** to build trust with existing followers.
3. **Engagement**: replying to comments and messages, conversation rather than broadcasting.
4. **Ads** for scale and a predictable flow of inquiries.

All of this works only with a clear path to an inquiry, **Direct** or your **website**, and someone who answers quickly.

## Profile optimization

Switch to a **professional account** (business or creator): it unlocks insights, contact buttons and advertising.

- **Name and username**: the profile name is used in Instagram search, so include a keyword, for example "Apartment Renovation Tashkent".
- **Bio**: what you do, for whom, what makes you different, and a call to action.
- **Link**: to your website, an inquiry form or a messenger. If you need several, use the built-in multiple links option or a landing page.
- **Contact buttons**: phone, email, address, "Message".
- **Highlights**: prices, reviews, work examples, how to order, FAQs.
- **Pinned posts**: your three strongest posts, selling for you.

## Reels and Stories strategy

**Reels** are shown to people who do not follow you, which makes them the main organic reach tool.

- The first seconds decide everything: show the result or state the problem right away.
- Make videos that work without sound: captions and on-screen text.
- Topics: answers to frequent questions, before and after, your process, customer mistakes.
- Post regularly, see which topics get watched to the end, and build on them.

**Stories** are seen mostly by followers. They are your daily touchpoint and warm-up:

- behind the scenes, process, team;
- polls, questions, sliders that drive reactions and suggest topics;
- reviews and replies to customers;
- a direct invitation to send a DM.

## Engagement

The algorithm considers how people interact with your account, and customers notice how you respond to them.

- Reply to comments and messages quickly, especially in the first hours after posting.
- Ask questions in captions and Stories.
- Leave meaningful comments on partners' and niche accounts.
- Partner with local creators and brands and use joint posts (Collab).

Buying followers or engagement backfires: reach drops and your statistics stop reflecting reality.

## Instagram Insights

Built-in analytics are available on professional accounts. What to watch:

| Metric | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Reach | How many unique accounts saw the content |
| Share of non-followers in reach | How well content attracts new people |
| Saves and shares | How useful a post is |
| Reels watch time | Whether the video holds attention |
| Profile activity | Link taps, calls, messages |

Once a month, compare topics and formats and do more of what leads to messages and link taps, not just likes.

## Boosting a post or Ads Manager

| | "Boost" button | Ads Manager |
|---|---|---|
| Effort | A couple of minutes in the app | Requires setup |
| Objectives | Limited set | Full range of campaign objectives |
| Targeting | Basic | Detailed, custom and lookalike audiences, retargeting |
| Reporting | Basic | Detailed, with Pixel and website conversions |
| Testing | None | A/B tests of creative and audiences |

**Boosting** is fine for quickly amplifying a post that already performs well. For a steady flow of inquiries, use **Ads Manager**: you can optimize campaigns for messages or conversions and exclude people who already contacted you.

## Driving people to an inquiry

- **To Direct**: ads with the Messages objective, a "Message" button on the profile, calls to action in Stories. Set up saved replies and an automatic greeting, and assign someone to respond.
- **To your website**: the profile link and Stories links, ads with the Traffic or Sales objective, **UTM tags** on links and the **Meta Pixel** on the site so inquiries appear in analytics.

Choose Direct if your product needs a consultation, and your website if people can decide on their own.

## FAQ

### Should I start with organic or paid?

Start with the profile and regular content: ads pointing to an empty profile perform worse, because visitors find nothing to trust. Once you know which posts spark interest, add ads.

### Do I need hashtags?

They can help, but they are not the main source of reach. A few precise topical and local hashtags are enough; content and keywords in captions and the profile matter more.

### Why did my reach suddenly drop?

Common reasons: a break in posting, repetitive content, a platform rule violation, unoriginal videos. Check your account status in settings and compare recent posts with the ones that worked before.
