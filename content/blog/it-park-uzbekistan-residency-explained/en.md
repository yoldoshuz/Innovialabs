## In short: what IT Park residency is

**IT Park Uzbekistan** is a state technology park that supports the country's IT industry. A company or sole proprietor granted **resident** status operates under a special regime: tax incentives, simpler rules for exporting services and access to support programs.

Residency is not tied to one building. You can work from any office in the country; what matters is the type of activity, not the address.

The regime is defined by legislation and changes from time to time. This article gives the general picture; for exact rates, deadlines and lists, see the [official IT Park website](https://it-park.uz).

## Who can become a resident

Residency is available to businesses whose core activity is IT. Typically:

- **software development** — web, mobile apps, enterprise systems;
- **IT outsourcing** and exporting IT services to foreign clients;
- **product startups** — SaaS, services, platforms;
- **data processing**, hosting, digital services;
- **BPO and related services**, if they are on the approved list.

The key condition is that revenue from approved activities makes up the main share of income. The list of activities is official, so check whether your work fits before applying.

## What resident status gives you

Exact figures depend on current rules, but in general the benefits look like this:

| Area | What a resident gets |
| --- | --- |
| **Company taxes** | A preferential regime instead of several standard taxes |
| **Employee taxes** | A reduced personal income tax rate |
| **Service exports** | Easier work with foreign clients and foreign currency revenue |
| **Hiring** | Simpler procedures for bringing in foreign specialists |
| **Ecosystem** | Accelerators, events, coworking spaces, investor connections |

For a small team, the main effect is usually a **predictable and lower tax burden**, which directly affects the price of development.

## What it means for clients

If you hire an IT Park resident to build your product, it can benefit you too:

- **Price.** A lower tax burden for the contractor often shows up in the project cost.
- **Transparency.** Residents are reviewed on entry and file reports, an extra signal that the company is real.
- **Cross-border work.** It is easier for a foreign client to sign a contract and pay in foreign currency.

Still, the status is not a quality guarantee. You need to check the portfolio, processes and contract yourself.

## How to get residency: the general process

1. **Check your activity** against the official list.
2. **Register a legal entity or sole proprietorship**, if you do not have one yet.
3. **Prepare documents** — a description of the project or activity, founding documents.
4. **Apply** through the IT Park online portal.
5. **Wait for the decision** and sign an agreement with IT Park.
6. **Keep the conditions** — file reports, pay the required contributions, maintain the share of IT revenue.

The application is online; an in-person visit is usually not needed.

## Common mistakes

- **Expecting incentives on everything.** The regime covers only income from approved activities. Reselling hardware or consulting outside the list may be taxed under general rules.
- **Forgetting the obligations.** Residents have reporting duties and regular contributions to the park; budget for them.
- **Relying on old articles.** Rates and rules have changed several times. The source of truth is the IT Park website and current regulations.
- **Mixing activities.** If a company has many non-IT lines of business, discuss the structure with an accountant in advance.

## FAQ

### Do I need an office in the IT Park building to become a resident?

No. Residency is a legal regime, not a lease. You can work from anywhere in Uzbekistan; the park's coworking spaces are an optional extra.

### Can a foreign company become a resident?

Usually a legal entity is registered in Uzbekistan first, and then it applies. Check the exact requirements for foreign founders on the official IT Park website.

### Can resident status be lost?

Yes, if the business stops meeting the conditions, for example the share of IT income drops below the requirement or reporting is violated. Track the conditions continuously, not only when joining.
