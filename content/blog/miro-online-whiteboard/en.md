## Short answer: what Miro is

**Miro** is a boundless online whiteboard where several people work at the same time in real time. You can add sticky notes, shapes, arrows, text, images, documents and tables, and embed links. Everyone sees colleagues' cursors and changes instantly.

Its main value is a **shared visual space** for work that does not fit well into documents and task trackers: generating ideas, mapping processes, discussing product structure.

## Brainstorming

The classic whiteboard scenario:

1. Write the question in a separate **frame** at the top.
2. Give participants 5–10 minutes of **silent ideation**, each writing ideas on their own sticky notes.
3. **Cluster** similar notes and label the groups.
4. Run a **vote** to pick ideas to develop.
5. Turn the chosen ideas into tasks with owners.

Silent ideation before discussion helps you hear more than just the loudest voices.

## Customer journey maps

A **customer journey map** works well as a table on the board: stages across the top (discover, compare, buy, use, recommend) and rows below:

- customer actions;
- touchpoints (website, bot, sales manager);
- thoughts and emotions;
- pain points and barriers;
- improvement ideas.

The board lets you attach screenshots, interview quotes and analytics links to each stage, so the whole team sees the journey end to end.

## Retrospectives

Simple formats work well for a sprint or project retro:

| Format | Columns |
|---|---|
| Start / Stop / Continue | Start, stop, continue |
| Mad / Sad / Glad | Frustrating, disappointing, pleasing |
| 4L | Liked, learned, lacked, longed for |

To get more honest answers, have participants write notes independently first and reveal them afterwards. End every retro with concrete actions, owners and dates, otherwise the meeting is just a chat.

## Planning on a board

- **Roadmap** — initiatives on a timeline by quarter or month.
- **Story mapping** — user activities across the top, features underneath by priority, release lines.
- **Prioritization** — a value/effort matrix where the team places tasks.
- **Process and architecture diagrams** — flowcharts and diagrams with connectors.

A board is great for discussion but does not replace a task tracker. Move agreements from the meeting into Jira, Trello or similar; Miro integrates with popular trackers.

## Templates

Miro has a large template library: retrospectives, journey maps, SWOT, roadmaps, user story maps, kanban, diagrams. Beyond the official ones there is a community catalog called **Miroverse**. A good habit is to build **your own templates** for recurring meetings so you do not set up the board from scratch every time.

## Facilitation features

These turn a board into a productive meeting rather than a mess of sticky notes:

- **Timer** for silent ideation and time-boxed discussion.
- **Voting** with a set number of votes per participant.
- **Bring everyone to me** and follow mode, so participants see the same area of the board.
- **Frames and presentation mode** to show the board step by step, like slides.
- **Comments and reactions** for asynchronous work between meetings.

Some features are only available on paid plans; check the current list on Miro's site.

## Free alternatives

Miro's free plan limits the number of editable boards. If that is not enough, consider:

- **FigJam** — Figma's whiteboard, convenient if your designers already work in Figma.
- **Excalidraw** — an open source board for quick hand-drawn diagrams that you can also self-host.
- **Microsoft Whiteboard** — a free board for Microsoft 365 users.
- **Lucidspark**, **Mural**, **Canva Whiteboards** — each has a free tier with limits.

Before choosing, check that the service is available in your country and where data is stored if boards will contain customer information.

## FAQ

### Can I invite someone without an account to a board?

Yes, a Miro board can be shared by link with view, comment or edit rights. Guest access depends on your plan and your team's security settings.

### How is Miro different from a task tracker?

A board is for thinking together: ideas, diagrams, discussions. A task tracker is for tracking tasks, deadlines and statuses. Teams usually use both.

### What is the simplest free alternative to Miro?

For quick diagrams and sketches, Excalidraw: it runs in the browser without signing up. For full workshops with templates and voting, FigJam is the closest match.
