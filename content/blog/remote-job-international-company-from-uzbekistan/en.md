## The short answer

Working remotely for a foreign company from Uzbekistan is realistic, but success depends on four things: **the right job sources**, **working-level English**, **understanding the hiring model** and **a ready, legal way to get paid**. If you do not sort out the last two in advance, an offer can fall through even after successful interviews.

## Where to find roles

- **LinkedIn** — the main channel. Use the Remote filter and check which countries the company can hire from.
- **Dedicated remote job boards**: sites listing remote roles, plus job boards run by specific technology communities.
- **Career pages of companies** that openly work as distributed teams.
- **Communities**: professional Telegram channels, Slack and Discord communities around technologies.
- **Referrals** from former colleagues already in international teams — often the fastest route.

Read the wording carefully: "Remote (US only)" or "EU timezone" means restrictions. Look for "Remote — worldwide", "EMEA" or an explicit mention of Central Asia.

## How to prepare

- **An English CV**: one or two pages, achievements framed as results ("cut build time", "launched the payment module"), not a list of duties.
- **A LinkedIn profile** in the same style, with open-to-work status.
- **GitHub or a portfolio** with clear READMEs.
- **Interview practice** in English: introducing yourself, walking through a past project, behavioural questions.
- **Time zone overlap**: decide in advance how many hours you can work in sync with the team.

## Hiring models

| Model | How it works | What matters for you |
|---|---|---|
| **Contractor** | A services agreement directly with the company | You handle taxes and paperwork; no paid leave or sick days by default |
| **EOR** (Employer of Record) | A third-party provider formally employs you in your country on the company's behalf | Closer to regular employment, but only if the provider supports Uzbekistan |
| **Via a contractor platform** | The company pays through a service such as Deel or Remote | Convenient payouts and documents; check which withdrawal methods are available |
| **Employment with relocation** | A standard employment contract in the company's country | A separate topic involving visas and moving |

Foreign companies most often hire specialists from Uzbekistan as **contractors**. Ask during the interview process exactly how you will be engaged — it affects taxes, payments and your rights.

## Receiving payments

Service availability changes, so check the current terms before signing:

- **SWIFT transfer** to a foreign-currency account at a bank in Uzbekistan — the basic, transparent option. Ask the bank about fees and the documents it will request.
- **Contractor platforms** — check which withdrawal methods are available to Uzbekistan residents.
- **Payment services** such as Payoneer — verify country support and withdrawal terms.

Keep your contract, invoices and payment confirmations: your bank may need them for currency control.

## Taxes for Uzbekistan residents

As a tax resident of Uzbekistan, you generally need to declare income from abroad. The main legal options:

- **IT Park residency** — a special regime for exporting IT services; check on the official IT Park website whether your activity qualifies.
- **Individual entrepreneur** registration.
- Declaring the income as a private individual.

Rates and conditions change, so **consult an accountant** before your first payment. This matters especially with EOR or a platform, since the model determines who withholds taxes and where.

## Common mistakes

- Applying to roles restricted to specific countries.
- Accepting an offer without clarifying the hiring model and payout method.
- Forgetting, as a contractor, to price in the lack of paid leave and the cost of taxes.
- Working without a contract and invoices.

## FAQ

### What level of English do I need?

Enough to handle interviews confidently, take part in calls and write clear messages. Perfect pronunciation is not required; clarity and the habit of asking clarifying questions matter more.

### Is EOR better than working as a contractor?

EOR is closer to traditional employment and removes some paperwork, but it is not available in every country. A contract is simpler for the company, but taxes and social protections become your responsibility.

### Should I register before my first salary?

It is better to sort out your legal status in advance: you will receive the first payment without stress and avoid issues with the bank and tax authority.
