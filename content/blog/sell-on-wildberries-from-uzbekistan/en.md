## The short answer: entering Wildberries from Uzbekistan

Wildberries accepts sellers from Uzbekistan directly — you do not need to open a company in another country to start. The path:

1. Register a **company or sole proprietorship** in Uzbekistan if you do not have one yet.
2. Create an account on the **seller portal**, choose your country of registration and enter your details.
3. Accept the **offer agreement** for your country and pass verification.
4. Create **product cards**.
5. Choose a **fulfillment model** and make your first shipment.
6. Reconcile **reports and payouts** and watch your metrics.

Keep in mind: goods from a marketplace warehouse may be shipped to buyers in several countries where it operates. Check document and marking requirements for every country your product will be sold in.

## Registration and documents

- **Registration data:** tax ID (STIR), registration certificate, director details.
- **Bank details** for payouts. The currency and account type are defined by the offer for your country.
- **Brand rights** — a trademark certificate or the owner's authorization.
- **Conformity documents.** Selling into EAEU countries means meeting their certificate and declaration rules, and some categories require mandatory product marking.
- **Taxes and currency control.** Agree with your accountant how marketplace revenue will be recorded — before the first payout, not after.

## Product cards

- **Subject and category** define the attribute set and the commission, so choose precisely.
- **Seller SKU and barcode** — each size and color has its own. The marketplace receives and tracks goods by them.
- **Title and attributes** in Russian — search and filters run on them.
- **Photos and video:** several angles, details, a size chart, the product in use.
- **Merging variants.** Combine colors of one model into a single card so reviews and rating are shared.

## Warehouse supply and logistics

| Model | How it works | When to choose |
|---|---|---|
| FBO (WB warehouse) | You create a shipment in the portal, book a warehouse and date, deliver labeled boxes. WB stores, packs and delivers | Fast movers, stable demand, fast delivery needed |
| FBS (your warehouse) | Stock stays with you. You pack orders from assembly tasks and drop them at a reception point | Wide assortment, bulky goods, testing new items |
| DBS (your delivery) | You store goods and deliver to the buyer yourself | Items that need special delivery or installation |

Practical tips:

- **Packaging and labels:** a barcode on every unit and a label on every box. Mistakes here cause most acceptance problems.
- **Choosing a warehouse.** Acceptance terms and costs differ by warehouse and date — check them before booking.
- **Stock distribution.** The closer goods are to buyers, the faster the delivery and usually the lower the logistics cost.
- **Shipping to warehouses abroad** is export: plan customs clearance, documents and lead times in advance.

## Payouts

The marketplace regularly issues **sales reports**. Your payout is not total sales — it is what remains after deductions:

- sales commission;
- logistics to the buyer and back for returns;
- warehouse storage;
- paid acceptance, if the chosen warehouse charges for it;
- penalties for rule violations;
- advertising, if paid from your balance.

Reconcile every report with your own records: units sold, units returned, what was deducted and why.

## Metrics to watch

| Metric | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Buyout rate | Share of orders actually purchased. A low rate eats margin through reverse logistics |
| Inventory turnover | How many days stock takes to sell. Slow turnover raises storage costs |
| Stock by warehouse | Risk of running out and losing search positions |
| Card conversion | Views → cart → order: where buyers drop off |
| Rating and reviews | Trust and search ranking |
| Ad spend share | Advertising cost as a share of revenue |
| Margin after deductions | Real profit per unit, not profit on paper |

## FAQ

### Do I need a Russian company to sell on Wildberries?

No. The marketplace works with sellers from Uzbekistan directly under the offer for your country. A company or sole proprietorship registered in Uzbekistan is enough.

### Should I start with FBO or FBS?

If the product is compact and sells fast, FBO gives faster delivery and more visibility. If you are testing the range or the goods are bulky, start with FBS and move bestsellers to the marketplace warehouse as sales grow.

### Why is my payout lower than my sales?

Commission, logistics, storage, returns, possible penalties and ads are deducted from revenue. Go through the sales report line by line to see which cost is eating your margin.
