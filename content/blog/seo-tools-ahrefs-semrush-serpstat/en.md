## The short answer

There's no universal winner. The choice depends on your task and market:

- **Ahrefs** — if backlink profiles and link-based competitor analysis matter most.
- **Semrush** — if you need an all-in-one suite: SEO, paid search, content, social, client reports.
- **Serpstat** — if the budget is tight and you mostly work with Russian-language search results.

For a small site in Uzbekistan, free tools are often enough, and a paid tool is added for a specific task.

## Database coverage: the key question for Uzbekistan

All three tools build their own keyword and backlink databases. For large markets the data is dense; for smaller ones it's noticeably thinner. Uzbekistan is a market where:

- some queries in Uzbek (Latin and Cyrillic) and Russian may be missing from the database;
- search volume estimates can differ significantly between tools;
- local Yandex results are not handled equally everywhere.

So don't choose based on marketing. Take 20–30 queries for which you know the real picture from Search Console and check which tool sees them more completely. Almost all of them offer a trial or a limited free mode.

## Feature comparison

| Task | Ahrefs | Semrush | Serpstat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Backlink analysis | Core strength | Good | Good |
| Keyword research | Good | Good | Good, convenient for Russian-language search |
| Competitor and content gap analysis | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Site audit | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Rank tracking | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Paid search, social | Limited | Broad | Partial |
| Yandex support | Limited | Limited | Better than the others |

Features change regularly, so check the current feature list on each vendor's site before buying.

## Pricing: what to look at

Exact prices change, so compare the pricing model rather than the numbers:

- **Limits.** How many projects, tracked keywords, report rows and daily lookups the plan includes.
- **Users.** Each extra team member often costs extra — significant for a team.
- **Add-ons.** Some features are sold separately on top of the main plan.
- **Monthly or annual.** Annual billing is usually cheaper but locks you in.

The most common overspend is buying a big plan "to grow into" and then using two reports.

## How to choose by team size

**Business owner or a solo marketer.** Start with free tools. Take a paid tool for a month for a specific task: keyword research before a launch or a competitor analysis.

**Small in-house team.** One shared tool. Pick the one that covers your markets best based on your own test, not on name recognition.

**Agency.** Client reporting, project counts and user seats matter. Agencies often choose Semrush or Serpstat and keep Ahrefs for deep backlink work.

## Free alternatives

- **Google Search Console** — real queries, clicks and positions of your site in Google.
- **Yandex Webmaster** — the same for Yandex, plus indexing diagnostics.
- **Yandex Wordstat** — query volumes in Yandex, including by region.
- **Google Keyword Planner** — keyword ideas and volume ranges; requires a Google Ads account.
- **Ahrefs Webmaster Tools** — free audit and backlink data for your own verified site.

Search Console, Webmaster and Wordstat together cover most of what a small site needs.

## FAQ

### Can I trust search volumes from these tools?

As a guide, yes; as an exact number, no. They're estimates, and the margin of error is larger in small markets. Compare queries relative to each other and take real traffic data from Search Console and Webmaster.

### Does a small site need a paid tool?

Usually not. Free tools are enough to start, and a paid tool makes sense for a short period and a specific task.

### Which tool is best for the Uzbek market?

There's no definitive answer — coverage changes. Test each one on queries you already know during a trial and pick the one that sees them most completely.
