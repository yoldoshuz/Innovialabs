## Short answer

- **Tilda** if you need a fast launch, a non-technical marketer will run the site, and your audience and payments are in the CIS.
- **Webflow** if you need a distinctive layout, structured content (blog, catalog, case studies), rich animation, and you are targeting a global market.

Both are visual builders with hosting included, but they work differently. Tilda is a set of ready-made blocks plus a free-form editor. Webflow is a visual interface on top of real HTML and CSS.

## Comparison at a glance

| Criterion | Tilda | Webflow |
|---|---|---|
| Design freedom | Ready blocks and Zero Block | Nearly any layout: flexbox, grid, classes |
| Learning curve | Low, learnable in a day | Requires HTML and CSS basics |
| CMS | Feeds and Store catalog | Collections with custom fields and references |
| Animation | Zero Block animations | Interactions: timelines, scroll, triggers |
| Hosting | On the platform, custom domain | On the platform, global CDN |
| Pricing model | Plan per account | Plan per site plus a workspace plan |
| Interface | Russian available | No Russian |

## Design freedom and learning curve

In Tilda a site is assembled from blocks, which is the main advantage for beginners: it is hard to make something truly bad. Freedom comes with **Zero Block**, but responsiveness there is adjusted by hand for each breakpoint.

In Webflow you are effectively writing layout: containers, spacing, **flexbox** and **grid**, reusable classes. This gives rule-based responsiveness instead of manual tweaking, and clean code. The flip side is that without understanding the CSS model Webflow is hard going, and inexperienced users quickly end up with a mess of classes.

## CMS and content

This is the biggest difference. In **Webflow CMS** you create your own collections — articles, case studies, products, team members — with the fields and relationships you need. One template page automatically generates a page for every item.

Tilda offers **Feeds** for blogs and the **Store catalog** for products. That covers typical needs, but arbitrary data structures and relationships between them are hard to build.

## Animation and interactivity

Webflow **Interactions** let you build multi-step animations triggered by scroll, click, hover or page load, and control them on a timeline. In Tilda, animation lives in Zero Block: entrance effects, scroll-based movement, step-by-step animation. Tilda is enough for most landing pages; for animation-heavy showcase sites Webflow is more comfortable.

## Hosting, pricing and payments

Both platforms host the site and issue SSL automatically. The pricing models differ:

- In Tilda you pay for an account plan that includes one or several sites.
- In Webflow you pay separately for **hosting each site** (Site plan) and, for teams, for the **workspace** (Workspace plan).

Prices change, so compare the full cost for your scenario on the official sites: number of sites, editors, whether you need a store.

**Local payments** often decide the matter. Tilda connects to many payment providers, including ones popular in the CIS, with the list depending on the country. Webflow Ecommerce relies on Stripe and PayPal, and Stripe is not available in every country of the region. Also check that you can pay for the subscription itself with a card the service accepts.

## Languages and multilingual sites

- **Interface:** Tilda has a Russian interface, Webflow does not. This matters for teams without English.
- **Multilingual sites:** Webflow has built-in Localization (a paid add-on) with separate page versions and CMS translation. In Tilda, extra languages are usually separate pages or projects linked by a language switcher.

## How to choose: a short checklist

1. Who will update the site? A marketer without technical experience — Tilda; a designer or developer — Webflow.
2. Is there structured content with dozens of similar pages? Yes — Webflow.
3. Do you need payments through local providers? Yes — check Tilda first.
4. Global market, with animation and SEO structure important? Probably Webflow.
5. Tight deadline and a simple landing page? Tilda.

A common mistake is choosing based on other people's showcase sites. A beautiful site can be built on either platform; choose based on **who will maintain it and how**.

## FAQ

### Which is better for SEO, Tilda or Webflow?

Both offer the basics: meta tags, page URLs, a sitemap and redirects. Webflow gives more control over structure and semantic markup, but rankings depend far more on content, speed and technical care than on the choice of builder.

### Can you move a site from Tilda to Webflow?

There is no automatic migration: pages have to be rebuilt and content moved manually or imported into the CMS. To keep search rankings, set up 301 redirects from the old URLs.

### Is Webflow suitable for an online store?

For a small store, yes, provided the supported payment providers work in your country. For a large catalog with inventory and complex logic, specialized platforms or custom development are the usual choice.
