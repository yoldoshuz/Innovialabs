## What a lead magnet is

A **lead magnet** is something useful you give away in exchange for a contact: an email, a phone number or a messenger subscription. The person gets a small problem solved; you get a chance to continue the conversation.

You need a magnet where a visitor is not ready to buy or book a consultation yet. Instead of "buy now" you say "here is something useful", and some of the visitors who would simply leave become leads.

## Types of lead magnets

| Type | Example | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Checklist or template** | "Online store launch checklist" | Quick to make and quick to use | Easy to download and forget |
| **Guide or roundup** | A guide to choosing equipment | Shows expertise | Takes time to read |
| **Calculator** | Renovation cost or payback estimate | Personal result, numbers right away | Needs development and honest logic |
| **Quiz** | "Which plan is right for you" | Engages and segments leads | A poor quiz is annoying |
| **Free trial or demo** | 14 days free, a demo account | People try the product itself | Needs onboarding or the trial is wasted |
| **Free consultation or audit** | Website review, project estimate | High lead quality in B2B | Expensive at scale, uses expert time |
| **Webinar or mini-course** | A lesson series in Telegram | Long contact and nurturing | Harder to produce |
| **Discount or promo code** | Discount on the first order | Works in B2C and e-commerce | Attracts bargain hunters |

## How a magnet fits the funnel

Magnets of different "warmth" suit different stages:

- **Top of the funnel** — the person is just recognizing a problem. Checklists, guides and quizzes fit. Goal: get the contact.
- **Middle** — the person compares solutions. Calculators, case studies and webinars fit. Goal: show you understand their situation.
- **Bottom** — the person is nearly ready. Trials, demos, consultations and audits fit. Goal: move to a deal.

A magnet is an entrance, not a finish line. After you get the contact you need a **next step**: an email sequence, bot messages, a call from a manager or an offer of a consultation. A magnet without follow-up just builds a list that does not sell.

## Criteria for a magnet that converts

1. **Solves one specific problem.** "Everything about marketing" fails; "A one-month content plan template" works.
2. **Connects to your product.** If you sell a CRM, a recipe magnet will collect many contacts and zero customers. Using the magnet should make wanting your product the logical next step.
3. **Delivers a quick win.** The value is visible in minutes, not after a week of reading.
4. **Clear from the headline.** People should instantly understand what they get and why.
5. **Filters out the wrong people.** A narrow topic and a qualifying question in the form beat broad reach. Fewer leads who can buy are better than many who cannot.
6. **Looks polished.** The magnet is the first impression of you; a sloppy document works as anti-advertising.
7. **Asks for minimal data.** An email or bot subscription is enough for a checklist; phone and company are appropriate for a consultation or audit.

## How to create one step by step

1. List frequent customer questions from sales and support.
2. Pick a question whose answer leads to your product.
3. Choose the format that answers it fastest.
4. Build a dedicated landing page or form with a single action.
5. Set up delivery and the follow-up sequence.
6. Track not only downloads but how many magnet leads reached a deal.

## Common mistakes

- A magnet for its own sake, with no link to the product.
- Material so long nobody finishes it.
- No follow-up after delivery.
- Judging success by contacts rather than sales.

## FAQ

### Which lead magnet works best for B2B?

Calculators, case studies, audits and consultations usually perform well: they show expertise and attract people with a real problem. Checklists help at the top of a B2B funnel but need more nurturing afterward.

### Can I give a magnet away without asking for a contact?

Yes, if your goal is reach and trust, such as an open article or calculator. But then it is content, not a lead magnet: you cannot continue the conversation.

### How do I know my lead magnet works?

Compare the share of visitors who get the magnet with the share of those leads who reach a sale. If you have many contacts and no sales, the magnet is too broad or unrelated to the product.
