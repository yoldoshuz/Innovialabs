## The short answer

**amoCRM** is a cloud CRM for sales teams. Its core idea is simple: every inquiry becomes a **deal** that moves through the stages of a **pipeline**, and the whole conversation with the customer lives inside that deal's card. Outside the CIS the product is developed under the **Kommo** brand.

amoCRM does not try to run your whole company. It answers sales questions: how many leads came in, where they are stuck, what each manager is doing and why deals are lost.

## How amoCRM works

### The pipeline comes first

The deals screen is a kanban board. Columns are sales stages (for example "New lead", "Qualified", "Proposal sent", "Payment"), cards are deals. A manager drags a card forward, and the system records how long the deal spent at each stage.

- You can run **several pipelines**: per product, region or for repeat sales.
- Each deal has a **contact** and a **company**, tasks, notes, call history and messages.
- Reporting is built on the pipeline: stage-to-stage conversion, loss reasons, plan vs actual per manager.

### Messengers and a unified inbox

amoCRM's strongest side is its **messenger integrations**. Telegram, WhatsApp, Instagram, website live chat and other channels connect so that messages land directly in the CRM. When a new person writes, the system can create a deal in the right pipeline automatically.

For businesses whose customers prefer to text rather than call, this is the key point: conversations no longer disappear into managers' personal phones.

### Digital Pipeline and automation

**Digital Pipeline** is a set of automatic actions tied to pipeline stages. When a deal enters a stage, the system can:

- create a task for the responsible manager;
- send the customer a message or email;
- change the owner or stage based on conditions;
- send a **webhook** to an external system;
- launch a **Salesbot** — a bot script that asks questions and writes answers into deal fields.

For custom logic there is a **REST API** and webhooks, plus a marketplace of widgets: ready-made integrations with telephony, mailing tools, payments and accounting systems.

## Pricing logic

Exact prices change, so the principle matters more than the numbers:

- you pay **per user** (each employee with access) for the chosen billing period;
- plans differ in **depth of features**: number of pipelines and fields, automation capabilities, advanced analytics and data limits;
- longer prepaid periods usually come with better terms.

On top of licences, budget for **paid widgets**, telephony and setup and integration work. Always check current terms on the official website.

## Who amoCRM suits

**Good fit:**

- companies with short to medium sales cycles: services, education, real estate, B2B sales with account managers;
- businesses where most leads arrive via messengers, social media and website forms;
- teams that need to launch quickly without a long implementation;
- owners who want a transparent pipeline and visibility into managers' work.

**Weaker fit:**

- when you need full **inventory, manufacturing or accounting** — that is ERP or 1C territory, and amoCRM only integrates with it;
- when you want a large corporate portal with tasks, documents and internal communication;
- when your sales process is so unusual that "a deal moves through stages" does not describe it.

## Common implementation mistakes

- **Copying someone else's pipeline.** Stages should reflect your real process, not a pretty template.
- **Too many required fields.** Managers start filling them in formally or work around the system.
- **Leaving channels unconnected.** If some leads bypass the CRM, your reports lie.
- **Automating chaos.** Describe the process first, then configure Digital Pipeline.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between amoCRM and Kommo?

It is the same product with the same logic. Kommo is the international brand, amoCRM is the name used in CIS markets. Plan details and the set of local integrations can differ.

### Can amoCRM connect to an online store or 1C?

Yes. Popular platforms have ready-made widgets, and custom systems use the REST API and webhooks: orders create deals, while statuses and payments sync back.

### How long does implementation take?

It depends on the number of pipelines, channels and integrations. A basic pipeline with messengers can be set up quickly, while a link to an accounting system and complex automation require project work and testing.
