## In short: what it is

**Generative AI** is a class of models that do not just analyze data but **create new content**: text, images, speech, music, video, code. You describe the task in words (a prompt), and the model generates a result similar to what it saw in training without copying a specific example.

The difference from "classic" AI is simple: classic AI answers "what is this?" (spam or not, which product to recommend), generative AI answers "make this" (write an email, draw a banner, voice a script).

## The main model families

| Content type | How models are usually built | Example tasks |
|---|---|---|
| Text and code | Large language models (LLMs) on the Transformer architecture | Customer replies, drafts, summaries, code |
| Images | Diffusion models that gradually "develop" a picture out of noise from a description | Illustrations, banner variants, product backgrounds |
| Audio and speech | Text-to-speech (TTS), speech recognition (ASR), music generation | Voice-overs, voice bots, call transcripts |
| Video | Diffusion and transformer models working on sequences of frames | Short clips, animating a still image |
| Multimodal | One model understands and produces several data types | Describe a photo, answer from a screenshot, parse a document |

The line between families is blurring: many modern models are **multimodal**, taking text and images as input and answering with text, voice or images.

## How it works, without formulas

All generative models learn from huge sets of examples and pick up patterns: how sentences are built, what objects look like, how speech sounds.

- A **text model** predicts the next piece of text, again and again.
- A **diffusion model** learns to remove noise from images; when generating, it starts from pure noise and gradually turns it into a picture that matches your request.
- **Speech synthesis** turns text into a sound wave with the right voice and intonation.

## What generative AI really does in business

- **Support:** a chatbot answers common questions from a knowledge base and hands complex ones to an agent.
- **Marketing:** drafts of posts, product descriptions and headline variants; visual concepts.
- **Sales:** personalized emails, call summaries in the CRM.
- **Documents:** extracting data from invoices and contracts, summarizing long texts.
- **Internal work:** search across company documents, coding help for developers.
- **Voice:** transcribing and analyzing calls, voicing training materials.

## What it cannot do or does unreliably

- **Guarantee facts.** A model can confidently invent a price, a date or a contract term.
- **Keep a brand exact without control.** Logos, text inside images and brand colors are often distorted.
- **Replace an expert.** Legal, medical and financial decisions need a specialist's review.
- **Work without your data.** The model does not know your products, prices and policies until you connect them.
- **Settle legal questions.** Rights to generated content, use of other people's voices and likenesses, personal data: check all of it against your local law and the service's terms.

## How to start without unnecessary risk

1. Pick **one narrow task** with a clear result, such as answering frequent questions.
2. Prepare **sources**: a knowledge base, a price list, policies.
3. Run a pilot with **human review** and collect examples of mistakes.
4. Define metrics: response time, share of resolved requests, staff workload.
5. Scale only what proved useful.

## FAQ

### How is generative AI different from ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is one specific product built on a language model. Generative AI is the umbrella term for all models that create content, including image generators, speech synthesis and video models.

### Can I use generated images in advertising?

Often yes, but the terms depend on the service and the law. Check the tool's license, avoid other companies' trademarks and recognizable people without consent, and refine the result by hand.

### Will generative AI replace employees?

More often it takes over the routine part of the work: drafts, sorting, transcription. People are still needed for review, difficult cases and decisions someone is accountable for.
