## GEO in plain terms

**GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** means optimizing a site for systems that do not just list links but **write the answer themselves** and cite sources. This covers Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Yandex's AI answers and Alice.

The goal of GEO is simple: when someone asks a question in your niche, the model should **find your site, understand it and cite it**.

## How AI search picks its sources

A simplified version of the process:

1. **Retrieval.** The system queries a search index, its own or a partner's, and finds pages for the query. A page that is not indexed cannot appear in the answer.
2. **Passage selection.** From those pages it picks the paragraphs that best answer the question.
3. **Synthesis.** A language model combines the passages into one answer and attaches source links.

The takeaway: AI search relies on **ordinary indexing**, but it favors text from which a precise, self-contained answer is easy to extract.

## GEO vs SEO: overlap and differences

| | Classic SEO | GEO |
|---|---|---|
| Goal | A position in the results list | A place in the answer text and a citation |
| Unit of evaluation | The whole page | A single paragraph or fact |
| Key signals | Relevance, links, technical quality | The same plus clear wording and brand mentions |
| Outcome | A click to the site | A mention, a link, sometimes a click |

**Overlap:** indexability, speed, quality content, trust. Without solid SEO, GEO barely works.

**Differences:** AI cares more about answers stated directly and briefly, and about your brand being described consistently across the web.

## What to do in practice

- **Answer first.** The opening paragraph of each section should give the direct answer, details after.
- **Write self-contained blocks.** Each paragraph should make sense without the rest of the article.
- **Use structure:** question headings, lists, comparison tables, definitions.
- **Be specific.** Steps, conditions and limits are what a model can quote.
- **Add markup.** Schema.org types such as Organization, Article, FAQPage and Product help make a page's subject unambiguous.
- **Check bot access.** Make sure `robots.txt` does not block AI search crawlers such as `OAI-SearchBot` or `PerplexityBot` if you want to appear in their answers.
- **Earn mentions.** Reviews, company profiles and publications on industry sites shape how a model "knows" your brand.
- **Keep content fresh.** Show an updated date and keep facts current.

## Common mistakes

- Treating GEO as a replacement for SEO and dropping technical work.
- Writing long intros where the answer gets lost.
- Mass-producing generic AI texts: such pages rarely become sources.
- Blocking every bot and then wondering why you never appear in AI answers.
- Expecting guarantees: answer generation changes constantly and cannot be fully controlled.

## FAQ

### Do I need to optimize separately for ChatGPT?

There is no special switch. The site must be accessible to the service's search crawler, well indexed and give clear answers. Most of the work is the same as good SEO.

### Does an llms.txt file help?

It is a proposed format describing a site for language models. Adding one is easy, but major AI search engines have not officially confirmed using it for ranking, so do not rely on it alone.

### How do I measure GEO results?

Track referrals from AI services in your analytics, manually check answers to key questions in your niche and monitor brand mentions.
