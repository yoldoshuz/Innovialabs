## The short answer

**Local SEO** is the work of making a business easy to find for people nearby. When someone searches for "dentist near me" or "phone repair Yunusabad", the search engine shows not only websites but also a **map pack** — a short list of businesses with address, rating, opening hours and a directions button.

Getting into this block is often more valuable than ranking first in regular results: it sits higher, and people immediately see where to go or whom to call.

## What local search results consist of

- **The map pack** — several businesses on a map right inside the search results.
- **Maps apps** — Google Maps, Yandex Maps, 2GIS, where people search directly.
- **Regular results** — websites and pages tied to a city or district.

The foundation is your **business listing**: Google Business Profile, Yandex Business, a 2GIS profile. Your website supports the listing but does not replace it.

## The three main ranking factors

Google explicitly names three groups of factors for local results:

| Factor | What it means | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Relevance** | how well the listing matches the query | precise categories, full service descriptions, current details |
| **Distance** | how far the business is from the user or the searched location | a correct address and service area |
| **Prominence** | how well known and trusted the business is | reviews, mentions, links, a quality website |

Distance is almost impossible to influence — nobody moves for SEO. So the real work is on relevance and prominence.

## What to do in practice

1. **Fill in your listings completely.** A real name without stuffed keywords, primary and secondary categories, address, phone, hours, photos, services.
2. **Keep your details identical everywhere.** Name, address and phone (known as **NAP**) must match on your site, in maps, directories and social networks.
3. **Work with reviews.** Ask satisfied customers to leave a review and reply to every review — including negative ones, calmly and to the point.
4. **Create location pages on your site.** If you have several branches, each needs its own page with address, map and description.
5. **Add structured data.** `LocalBusiness` markup from Schema.org helps search engines understand your address, hours and business type.
6. **Keep information fresh.** Holiday hours, new services, recent photos — an outdated listing loses trust.

## Who benefits most

- **Businesses with a physical location**: cafes, salons, clinics, car services, shops, gyms.
- **On-site services in one city**: repairs, cleaning, delivery, installation.
- **Multi-branch companies** that want to send customers to the nearest location.
- **B2B with local demand**: lawyers, accountants, suppliers that people look for by city.

An online service with no office and customers across the country gets less from local SEO — regular SEO matters more for it.

## Common mistakes

- **Keywords in the listing name.** "Best cheap dentist Tashkent" instead of the real name breaks map guidelines and can get the listing suspended.
- **Different phones and addresses** across directories — the search engine loses confidence in your data.
- **Fake reviews.** Platforms detect suspicious reviews and may remove them or restrict the listing.
- **Being listed in only one system.** Your customers use different maps — be present wherever they search.

## FAQ

### Do I need a website if I have a maps listing?

A listing can bring customers on its own, but a website strengthens it: it gives details about services, prices and branches and helps search engines judge how prominent the business is.

### Can I appear in the map pack without a physical address?

For service-area businesses, maps usually let you set a service area and hide the address. Rules differ between platforms, so check the requirements of each one.

### How fast does local SEO work?

Listing changes appear quickly, but ranking growth depends on competition in your niche and area, and on how fast reviews and mentions accumulate.
