## What SEO is, in plain words

**SEO (Search Engine Optimization)** is the set of work that helps a website appear higher in the organic (unpaid) results of Google and Yandex. The goal is not ranking for its own sake, but getting visitors who are looking for exactly what you sell or know.

A search engine solves one problem: show a person the most useful answer. SEO helps it understand that your page is that answer.

## What SEO influences

- **Visibility** — which queries the site appears for and at what positions.
- **Traffic** — how many people arrive from search without you paying per click.
- **Visitor quality** — people arrive with a specific task, so they convert more often.
- **Trust** — a site that consistently shows up for relevant queries looks reliable.

SEO does not directly decide whether someone buys. The product, price, design and usability do that.

## The three pillars of SEO

| Pillar | What it includes | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Technical** | speed, indexing, mobile version, URL structure, sitemap, robots.txt | so the search engine can find and read your pages correctly |
| **Content** | pages built around real queries, headings, meta tags, page structure | so the page answers the question better than others |
| **Links** | mentions and links from other sites, reviews, directories | so the search engine sees that others trust you |

When one pillar is weak, the others work worse. A great text will not help if the page is blocked from indexing. A fast site will not rise if it lacks the content people search for.

## How it works in practice

1. **Keyword research.** Find out which words customers use to look for your services.
2. **Site structure.** Each group of queries gets its own page.
3. **Technical audit.** Remove errors that hurt indexing and speed.
4. **Content.** Write pages that answer the query fully and clearly.
5. **External signals.** Earn mentions on relevant sites, maps and directories.
6. **Analytics.** Track positions and traffic in Google Search Console and Yandex Webmaster, and adjust the plan.

## Realistic expectations

- **SEO is not fast.** First changes usually show up within a few months, and stable results take longer. Timing depends on competition in your niche, the site's age and its starting condition.
- **No one can guarantee positions.** Search algorithms are closed and keep changing. A promise of "number one in a month" is a red flag.
- **Results compound.** A good page keeps bringing traffic long after it was published.
- **It is a process, not a one-off job.** Competitors keep working and search engines keep updating their rules.

## When a business needs SEO

SEO makes sense if:

- your customers **search for the service** (repairs, legal help, education, B2B services, online stores);
- you are ready to wait for results and invest regularly;
- the site has something to show: clear service pages, prices or terms, contacts.

SEO can wait if:

- there is almost no search demand — the product is new and people do not know to look for it yet;
- you need sales this week — ads will work faster;
- there is barely a site, or it will be fully rebuilt soon.

## Common mistakes

- **Writing "for robots"** by repeating keywords. Modern search engines judge usefulness, not keyword density.
- **Buying links in bulk.** It risks penalties.
- **Starting SEO after launch.** Structure and technical basics are cheaper to build in during development.
- **Watching only positions.** Traffic and leads matter more.

## FAQ

### Can I do SEO myself?

Yes, the basics — headings, meta descriptions, content for real queries, registering in Search Console and Webmaster — need no specialist. A technical audit and strategy in a competitive niche usually require experience.

### How is SEO different from search ads?

Ads bring traffic immediately, as long as you pay for clicks. SEO brings unpaid traffic, but results arrive more slowly and last longer.

### Do I need SEO if I have Instagram and Telegram?

Social media and search reach different audiences. If customers look for your service on Google or Yandex, skipping SEO means losing that share of demand.
