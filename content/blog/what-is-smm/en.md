## The short answer

**SMM (social media marketing)** is marketing on social networks and messengers: Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Telegram, VK and any other platform where your audience spends time.

It is not "running a page and posting". A social media marketer's job covers five areas:

1. organic content;
2. community management;
3. paid social advertising;
4. influencer work;
5. analytics and reporting.

In small teams, one person does all of this. In larger ones, there are separate content managers, paid social specialists, community managers and influencer managers.

## Organic content

These are publications your audience sees without paid impressions: posts, stories, short videos, live streams.

The work includes:

- **strategy** — who you speak to and why, which topics and formats, how the brand sounds;
- **content plan** — what goes out, where and when;
- **production** — copy, shooting, design, editing (often together with a designer and videographer);
- **platform adaptation** — what works on Telegram does not always work on TikTok.

Organic reach on many platforms is limited by algorithms, so content alone is usually not enough for growth.

## Community management

**Community management** means talking with your audience:

- replying to comments and messages;
- handling complaints and negativity — quickly, calmly, moving the resolution into private messages;
- passing leads from direct messages to sales or the CRM;
- moderating chats and groups.

Slow replies to direct messages are one of the most common reasons social media leads get lost.

## Paid social

**Paid social** means paid impressions targeted by interests, demographics, behaviour, lookalike audiences or your own customer lists. What matters:

- choosing the campaign objective: reach, traffic, leads, messages, sales;
- creatives and copy that stop the scroll;
- correct pixel and event setup so the algorithm learns from the right actions;
- constant testing of audiences and ads.

Ads are usually what produces a fast and measurable flow of leads.

## Influencer work

**Influencer marketing** means placements with creators your audience trusts. The work involves:

- finding creators with a relevant audience, not just a large follower count;
- checking statistics: where followers come from, whether they are bought, what engagement looks like;
- briefing, approvals, publication control;
- tracking results with promo codes, UTM links or dedicated landing pages.

Note that in many countries sponsored content must be labelled as advertising.

## Reporting

A good SMM report shows more than likes. Split metrics into levels:

| Level | Example metrics |
|---|---|
| Reach | impressions, reach, follower growth |
| Engagement | reactions, comments, saves, shares, video completion |
| Actions | clicks, messages, leads, cost per lead |
| Money | sales and revenue from social (via CRM and UTM) |

The closer a metric is to money, the more it matters to the business.

## What SMM can and cannot do

**It can:**

- make the brand recognizable and understandable;
- build trust through regular useful content and real conversations;
- bring leads and sales, especially combined with ads;
- give you direct customer feedback.

**It cannot:**

- sell a product the market does not need;
- make up for slow replies and weak sales follow-up;
- deliver stable sales from organic posts alone within a couple of weeks;
- work without a budget for ads and content production if the goal is fast growth.

## FAQ

### Which social networks should a business be on?

Wherever your audience spends time. It is better to run one or two platforms well than to be present everywhere on paper.

### How is a social media marketer different from a paid social specialist?

A paid social specialist sets up and optimizes paid ads. A social media marketer is responsible for the overall presence: content, community and sometimes ads and influencers.

### When should I expect results from SMM?

Paid ads can bring inquiries soon after launch if everything is set up correctly. Organic growth and awareness build gradually, and the timeline depends on the niche, the content and consistency.
