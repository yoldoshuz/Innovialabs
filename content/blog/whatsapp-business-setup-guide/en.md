## Short answer

WhatsApp Business is a free app for small companies. The basic setup takes an evening and comes down to six steps: **profile, catalog, labels, greeting and away messages, quick replies, short link**. Almost everything lives in the app settings under "Business tools".

## Before you start: which number

- One number works either in regular WhatsApp or in WhatsApp Business. When you switch on the same phone, the app offers to move your chats.
- Use a **dedicated work number**, not an employee's personal one: if that person leaves, customer conversations stay with the company.
- You can link a computer and other devices to the account, so replying from a laptop is easy.

## Step 1. Business profile

The profile is the first thing a customer sees when opening the chat. Fill in every field:

- **name** — the way customers know you, no extra words or emojis;
- **category** — tells people what you do;
- **description** — one or two sentences: what you do, for whom, where;
- **address and business hours** — the hours are used later by the away message;
- **website, email, social links**;
- **logo** — square and readable in a small circle.

## Step 2. Catalog

The catalog shows products and services right inside the chat. For each item, add a photo, name, price, short description, website link and item code. Group items into **collections**: "New", "Delivery", "Services".

Customers can open the catalog from your profile, build a cart and send it to you as a message. Managers, in turn, share an item card in a couple of taps instead of describing it by hand.

## Step 3. Labels

Labels are colored tags for chats. The app has a default set, but it is better to match it to your sales process:

- New customer
- Waiting for reply
- Awaiting payment
- Paid
- Returning customer

Filter chats by label and within a minute you see who needs a payment reminder and whom to thank.

## Step 4. Greeting and away messages

The **greeting message** is sent automatically when a customer writes for the first time or after a long pause in the conversation. A good greeting is short and suggests the next step:

```text
Hello! This is Example Store. Tell us what you are looking for or open the catalog in our profile. We reply from 9:00 to 19:00.
```

The **away message** can be sent always, on a custom schedule, or only outside the business hours from your profile. For both messages you choose the audience: everyone, only people not in your contacts, or specific people.

Be honest: don't promise "we reply in 5 minutes" if nobody answers on weekends.

## Step 5. Quick replies

Quick replies are templates you call with `/` in the input field. Create them for recurring questions:

- `/price` — prices and terms;
- `/delivery` — delivery and timing;
- `/pay` — payment methods;
- `/address` — address and landmark.

You can attach a photo or video to a template. Reread the texts regularly: an outdated price in a template quickly turns into a dispute.

## Step 6. Short link and QR code

Customers don't need to save your number — a link is enough. Business tools include a short link with a QR code and a prefilled message. You can also build the link yourself: the number in international format without "+", spaces or dashes:

```text
https://wa.me/998901234567?text=I%20want%20to%20ask%20about%20delivery
```

Spaces in the text become `%20`. Put the link in your social media bio, on the website, in your Telegram channel, on packaging and business cards.

## Common mistakes

- **a wall-of-text greeting** — customers skip it and ask their question anyway;
- **a catalog without prices or with outdated items** — creates extra questions;
- **labels created but never applied** — then they are just colored dots;
- **a manager's personal number** — the whole conversation history leaves with them.

When you have many operators, need CRM integration, a chatbot or bulk notifications, the app is no longer enough — the next step is the WhatsApp Business Platform with its API.

## FAQ

### How much does WhatsApp Business cost?

The app itself is free. What can be paid is working through the API platform, where certain message types are billed.

### Can I use it from a computer and several devices?

Yes, the account can be linked to a computer and other devices. That is enough for a small team, but a large sales department is better served by the API connected to a CRM.

### How is WhatsApp Business different from regular WhatsApp?

The business version adds a company profile, catalog, labels, auto-replies, quick replies and a short link. For the customer the chat looks the same, but they can see they are writing to a business.
