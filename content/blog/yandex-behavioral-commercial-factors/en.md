## The short answer

**Behavioral factors** are signals about how people interact with search results and your site: do they click your snippet, do they bounce straight back to search, do they find what they came for. **Commercial factors** are signs that a site is a trustworthy, complete seller: prices, contacts, payment and delivery options, assortment, legal information.

Yandex does not publish its formulas, but the logic is clear: for commercial queries, sites where buying is easier and safer tend to rank higher. So you improve the site itself, not the "signals".

## Behavioral signals that matter

- **Snippet click-through.** Your title and description should answer the query and set you apart from competitors.
- **Returning to search.** If someone leaves at once and clicks the next result, the page probably did not solve their task.
- **Task completion on the site.** The visitor found the product, price and terms, called or submitted a request.
- **Mobile usability.** Slow loading and awkward layouts quickly turn into bounces.

### How to improve behavioral signals

1. Check that the page matches the **search intent**: a "buy" query needs a catalog with prices, not an article.
2. Put the essentials on the first screen: price, availability, a clear action button.
3. Speed up loading and test the layout on a phone.
4. Rewrite titles and descriptions with specifics instead of generic phrases.
5. Use **Webvisor** and click maps in Yandex Metrica to see where people get lost.

## Commercial factors: a trust checklist

| Element | What to check |
|---|---|
| Prices | Shown on product cards and in the catalog, not "price on request" everywhere |
| Contacts | Phone, address, hours, messengers, map |
| Assortment | A full catalog with filters and sorting |
| Delivery and payment | Separate pages with clear terms |
| Legal details | Company name, registration details, privacy policy |
| Warranty and returns | Clear exchange and return conditions |
| Reviews | Real customer reviews, including on external platforms |
| Business profile | A complete listing in Yandex Business |

Compare your site with the top results for your queries: what do they have that you do not? This is the most practical way to find gaps.

## Common mistakes

- **Faking behavioral signals.** Yandex fights this and can demote the site. The risk far outweighs any short-term gain.
- **Contacts only in the footer.** Phone and messenger should be visible right away.
- **Empty product cards.** No photos, specs or price, and the visitor leaves.
- **Intrusive pop-ups** on the first screen, especially on mobile.
- **No "Delivery", "Payment" or "About" pages.** For commercial results these are the bare minimum.

## How to prioritize

1. First fix what stops people from buying: prices, contacts, buttons, the mobile version.
2. Then add trust elements: delivery terms, warranties, company details, reviews.
3. After that, work on snippets and content using Metrica data.

Changes in behavioral signals do not show up instantly, so evaluate results over several weeks and compare them with the period before the changes.

## FAQ

### Can I boost rankings quickly by faking behavioral signals?

It sometimes gives a short-lived effect, but Yandex detects artificial signals and may apply sanctions. For a business planning to stay long term, the risk is not worth it.

### Do informational sites need commercial factors?

Less so. For a blog, answer completeness and readability matter more, but contacts and information about the author and company still build trust.

### How do I know a page is not solving the user's task?

Look at bounce rate, page depth and Webvisor recordings for specific landing pages in Yandex Metrica. If people leave quickly without scrolling, the content does not match their expectations.
