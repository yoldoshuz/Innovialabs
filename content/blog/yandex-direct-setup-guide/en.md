## The short answer: launch order

1. Install **Yandex Metrica** on your site and set up goals (lead, call, purchase).
2. Create a **Yandex Direct** account with a Yandex ID and choose your country and currency.
3. Create a **Unified Performance Campaign** (UPC): search and the Yandex Advertising Network (YAN) in one campaign.
4. Link your Metrica tag and choose the goals to optimize for.
5. Set location, keywords, negative keywords, ads and a bidding strategy.
6. Top up the balance and submit the campaign for moderation.

## Step 1. Account and preparation

- Sign in to Direct with a company Yandex ID, not an employee's personal email: access is easier to hand over later.
- **The currency is chosen when the account is created** and cannot be changed afterwards, so decide in advance which currency suits payments and reporting.
- Payment methods and paperwork depend on the advertiser's country — check the available options in the interface before launch.
- If a contractor runs your ads, grant them **representative access** instead of sharing a login and password.

## Step 2. UPC or simplified settings

| | Unified Performance Campaign | Campaign Wizard |
|---|---|---|
| Where | Expert mode | Simplified mode |
| Control | Bids, placements, keywords, adjustments | Minimal settings, the algorithm decides |
| Best for | People ready to dig in and analyze | A quick start on a small budget |

A UPC can run on both search and the network, or on just one of them. A common practice is to **split search and network into separate campaigns**: they have different click costs, different user intent and need different ads.

## Step 3. Search and network

**Search** shows ads to people who are looking for your product right now. What matters here:

- **keywords** — research them in Wordstat filtered by your target region;
- **negative keywords** — cut irrelevant queries such as "free", "download", "jobs";
- **match operators**: `"quotes"` fix the number of words, `!` fixes the word form, `+` fixes function words, `[]` fixes word order.

**YAN** shows ads on partner websites and apps. Banners and text-and-image ads work here, and keywords act more as a hint for finding the audience. On the network, images and placement cleanup matter more: regularly exclude sites and apps that deliver clicks without conversions.

## Step 4. Linking Metrica

Without Metrica, Direct does not know which clicks turned into leads. In the campaign settings:

- enter your Metrica tag number;
- choose **goals for optimization** — one or two main ones, not everything at once;
- keep **link tagging for Metrica** enabled so visits are correctly matched to clicks;
- for pay-per-conversion strategies, make sure the goal fires reliably and without duplicates.

Test your goals before launch: submit a test lead and confirm the conversion shows up in Metrica reports.

## Step 5. Location: Uzbekistan and CIS

- Choose where ads are shown: the whole country, specific cities (such as Tashkent) or several countries.
- **Extended geo-targeting** shows ads to people elsewhere if their query mentions your region. For local services this may be unnecessary — check the setting.
- For several countries, use separate campaigns: budgets, ad language and bids are easier to manage that way.
- Consider your audience's language: in Uzbekistan it makes sense to check demand in both Russian and Uzbek and prepare ads for each.
- Assess Yandex's share among your audience: in Uzbekistan a large part of search goes through Google, so Direct is often combined with Google Ads.

## Common mistakes

- Launching without Metrica goals and optimizing for clicks.
- Search and network running identical ads with mixed statistics.
- No negative keywords, so budget goes to irrelevant queries.
- Too many goals in optimization, so the algorithm learns from minor actions.
- Daily strategy edits that disrupt learning.

## FAQ

### How much budget do I need to start?

There is no exact figure: it depends on competition in your niche, click costs and the chosen strategy. For conversion-optimized strategies, the budget should be enough for the campaign to collect conversions every week.

### Which should a beginner choose: UPC or Campaign Wizard?

If you have no time to dig in, the Campaign Wizard. If you want to understand what you pay for and manage placements and keywords, a UPC in expert mode.

### Does Yandex Direct work without a website?

Yes, you can send traffic to a business profile or a messenger, for example. But a website with Metrica gives the algorithm more data to optimize on.
